## Failure mechanism

Two deterministic rule weaknesses are directly demonstrated:

1. **Default Kraken symbol aliases are matched case-insensitively in page titles.** This lets common words / word components such as `Near`, `Term`, `Sky`, and `grass` act as crypto symbols when ordinary prose happens to contain them.
2. **The crypto-title anchor includes generic words such as `coin`.** Ordinary monetary uses can satisfy the context gate even when the article is unrelated to cryptocurrency.

## Required corrective direction

The next candidate matching definition must remain deterministic and should, at minimum:

- distinguish default Kraken symbol aliases from independently approved long-form aliases;
- require case-sensitive uppercase symbol evidence for default symbol-only page-title matches;
- replace generic title anchors (`coin`, `coins`, `token`, `tokens`, etc.) with a narrower crypto-specific anchor vocabulary;
- preserve exact long-form approved alias matching;
- regenerate the full Q2 Stage 3 outputs and repeat the bounded review before `CFA-S3-006` can PASS.
